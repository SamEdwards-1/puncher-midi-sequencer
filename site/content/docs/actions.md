## The four actions {#actions}

| Action | Key | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hold | H | Keeps the current step while the voices continue playing. |
| Sync | Y | Makes the selected voice play at the sequencer's pace, one note per step. |
| Flip | F | Swaps the grid's rows and columns from the next step onward. |
| Transpose | T | Applies the sequencer's Transpose amount to new notes. |

Hold the button or key for as long as you want the action. With **Latch** enabled, one press turns it on and the next turns it off. These keys do not take over while you type in an input.

## Try a held variation {#try-a-variation}

Start a sequence and hold **H** on a chord you want to stay on. The voices keep running their patterns over it. Hold **T** to apply the configured transposition, then release both to continue.

To make an action part of a patch, use its gear to add [modulation](/docs/modulation). An action envelope overrides the button on steps that contain it; other steps use the button's state.
